ABOUT UPTICK LOCAL

Local businesses have always been media. Nobody treated them that way.

Every day, people walk into the same handful of neighborhood businesses and spend real minutes standing still. Uptick Local turns those minutes into a media network: a free screen for the business, a real audience for advertisers, and a reason for nearby locations to promote each other.

WHAT WE BELIEVE
01

The screen should pay for itself

A host should never be asked to fund media infrastructure.

02

Audience beats address

Where a screen sits matters only because of who is standing in front of it.

03

Neighbors should promote neighbors

The network is worth more to a host than any single screen could be.

04

Say only what is true

No invented numbers, no guaranteed returns, no coverage we cannot confirm.
