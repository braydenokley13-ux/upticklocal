Host questions,
answered

What does the screen cost?+

Nothing. Hardware, installation, content updates, and support are covered by Uptick. We earn by selling airtime to local advertisers.

What plays on it?+

Your promotions lead the loop, followed by vetted neighborhood advertisers. Nothing that competes with your business, ever.

How much work is it for me?+

A power outlet and Wi-Fi. Send us a special when you have one; we design the slide and keep the loop fresh.

Do I earn from the ads?+

Yes — as advertising sells in your area, hosts share in the revenue. Your screen also gets cross-promoted on other screens nearby.
