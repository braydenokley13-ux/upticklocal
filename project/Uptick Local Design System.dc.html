SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH

The Uptick Local design system.

Derived from one idea: a local business becomes a media channel, and channels are made of frames in a run. The band, the tick and the counter-height screen are the whole visual language. Everything below is downstream of that.

01 · PRINCIPLES

Five rules that decide everything else

P1

The business model is the graphic

Locations, screens, audiences, networks and campaigns are drawn as squares on a band. If a graphic cannot be traced back to that chain, it does not belong.

P2

Premium comes from restraint, energy from scale

Two surfaces, one accent, square corners, hairline rules. The excitement is carried by type size and full-bleed imagery, never by decoration.

P3

Motion explains, never entertains

Every scroll-linked moment has a sentence it is making. If you cannot write the sentence, delete the animation.

P4

Three audiences, never blurred

Host, advertiser and screen-network partner always have separate doors. No page tries to convert two of them at once except the homepage split.

P5

Only claim what is verified

No screen counts, no coverage maps, no revenue figures, no testimonials until they exist. Unknown numbers are simply absent — the layout must look complete without them.

AVOID BECAUSE IT MAKES UPTICK GENERIC: rounded card grids, gradient blobs, glass panels, three-up feature rows on every section, stock photography of people shaking hands, neon glows, icon-per-benefit layouts.

WHEN TO GO CINEMATIC

Three moments per site, no more: the hero, the six-beat system story, and the audience selector. Everything else reveals and stops.

REDUCED MOTION

Pins release, all sequences become static stacked layouts with every layer already visible, filmstrips freeze, crossfades become instant swaps driven by the step indicator. No information is only available through movement.

20 · ACCESSIBILITY

Non-negotiable floors

CONTRAST

Body text 4.5:1 minimum, display 3:1. Amber is never text on paper — Amber Ink #8F5E11 is. Network hues never carry text on either surface; their text-safe variants do.

FOCUS

2px amber outline at 3px offset on every interactive element, on both surfaces. Focus is never removed, and never relies on the hover treatment.

TARGETS

44px minimum, 56px for primary buttons and mobile rows. Select cards are full-width blocks on mobile. Nav items get 12px of vertical padding beyond the label.

TYPE FLOORS

Body 16px minimum. Mono labels 9px minimum and only for short uppercase strings — never a sentence. Fine print is 12px mono at 1.7 leading.

NOT COLOUR ALONE

Networks and categories are always named in words beside their square. Selected states carry a fill change and a label change, not only a hue. Errors carry text, not just a red rule.

MOTION & FORMS

prefers-reduced-motion releases every pin and reveals all layers. Labels are real labels, always visible, programmatically associated. Errors are announced and focus moves to the first invalid field.

BUILD ORDER

Tokens, then the device component, then the network field graphic. Those three carry the brand; everything else is layout. Ship the homepage with real reveals and only then add the three pinned scenes.

PHOTOGRAPHY

Every diagonal hairline fill is a slot with a written brief in it. Ship with the fills rather than placeholder stock — an honest empty frame is better than a wrong photograph, and the layout is designed to survive it.

OPEN ITEMS

Approved product photography for the 21-inch unit. Confirmed geography for the selector. Host revenue language reviewed by counsel. Any number that appears anywhere needs a source before it ships.

26 · COMPOSITION — V2 REVISION

Fewer components on screen at once

Stakeholder direction after round one: the system must enable editorial composition, not card grids. These rules govern every page assembled from the components above.

C1 · ONE IDEA PER VIEWPORT

At any scroll position, one dominant message. If three headlines, six cards, and four CTAs are visible together, the section is redesigned — not shrunk.

C2 · PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE

The homepage sells the choice; interior pages sell the detail. Feature lists, FAQs, category grids, and process steps live on /locations, /advertisers, and /networks.

C3 · TWO DOORS

Every entry surface resolves to one decision: I have a location, or I want to advertise. Both doors visible above the fold, styled as major panels, never buried in a button row.

C4 · REAL WORLD FIRST

Field photography of installed units leads; device renders supplement. Keep the shelves, freezers, and counters — frame them intentionally with crops, scrims, and mono captions.

C5 · MOTION BUDGET

One motion idea per section: a sticky scene, a parallax layer, or a reveal — never all three. Reduce content before reducing motion ambition.
