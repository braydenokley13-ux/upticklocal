## Score

**8.8 / 10** against the boutique-commercial bar. A subjective director
assessment of the delivered master.

Up from 8.4, and the gain is strategic rather than cosmetic: the film now sells
a recurring audience instead of a free coffee, shows the acquisition mechanism,
and closes the loop with a second redemption.

**It is not the 9+ the brief asked for, and I am not going to call it one.**
The reason is precise and it is the same reason three times over: the Higgsfield
service was unreachable for this entire production run, so three moments that
should be *covered* are instead *implied*.

1. **The customer never visibly chooses.** A rack focus onto a sign is a good
   substitute for a decision. It is not a decision. One clip of him at the wash,
   looking up, would move this film more than any other single thing.
2. **There is no pump.** Fuel is a canopy, a sound and a receipt. It works, but
   the physical progression pump → door is asserted rather than shown, and that
   progression is half of what makes the offer clever.
3. **Two of the three network businesses are names, not places.** The network
   reads as real because the one screen we do see is convincing; a second host
   would make it read as infrastructure.

Everything else on the brief's list of eight strategic fixes is fully delivered.
Seven of eight is honest work. The eighth needs a camera.
