Exact persistent qualification: **“Illustrative customer journey. Outcomes are not guaranteed.”** It remains on both full film and teaser. On the final 1920×1080 layout it is 22 px, outside the picture in the lower matte; inspect legibility at delivery size before lock. It should not be treated as a cure for an otherwise misleading claim.

No web-sourced commercial-performance claims were introduced. All offer and event logic descends from the user's repository fixture; historical totals still pass its arithmetic check.
