# Gate B · the look, on final frames

_Gate A is creative and runs on the proxy lane: camera, blocking, pacing, cuts, world crossings
(`film/review/PROOF.md`, three proof cuts, all passed). **Gate B is this file.** It runs on
1280×720 final frames only, because judging finish from a 640×360 plate upscaled three times is
precisely the error the two-gate structure exists to prevent — measured once already, when a
reframe scored 8.0 on the proxy and failed at final resolution._

Each plate is read as it lands, three to nine frames across its own arc, at 1:1 where a detail is
in question. A plate passes when nothing in it reads as a low-poly explainer, an archviz frame, a
tech demo, or UI pasted over a render — and when the product inside the shot is lit by the room
rather than laid on top of it.
