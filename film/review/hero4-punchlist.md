## Verdict

**5/10.** The beats are all present and in roughly the right order, but the shot currently reads as an iMessage transcript with a ghost screenshot floating behind it — which is the first item on the rejected list. Two cream rounded-rects with 14px radii and drop shadows, a "…" typing indicator at f24, and a "Thursday, 6:52 PM" timestamp at f48 are literal chat-app chrome; remove those four things and the shot stops being a mockup. Provenance fails harder: the plan sheet arrives at ~f72 at roughly 18% opacity behind the message bubble and the question line, its header is ~14px at 1920 and unreadable, and the first sampled frame of the three words is f108 — already mid-arc, already detached, already tiny (~22px). Nothing shows the words *leaving* the sheet, so "grounded, not invented" is asserted by the copy ("from Joe's approved offer details," 18px) rather than proven by the image. The serif italic voice, the mint discipline, and the silence are genuinely good. Fix the sheet's legibility and the detach and this goes to 8.
