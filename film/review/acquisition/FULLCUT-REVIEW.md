### The 20-second problem

Five of six checkpoints are clear. The one that is not is the most dangerous.

At twenty seconds the film has shown **one** placement, at **one** business,
followed by a phone. Everything that separates Uptick from a QR code — the same
installed unit at Quick Lube and at Ridge Tire, one placement grammar repeated
across trades — does not arrive until around forty-eight seconds. So for the
first third of the film the DIY reading is not merely available, it is the most
economical explanation of what the viewer has seen.

This is a structural finding about the edit, not a defect in any shot, and it is
the most valuable thing watching the cut has produced.

## Revision 1 — applied, and re-watched

The network seed was moved before the journey. A two-second act at 11-13
seconds shows the same installed unit at Quick Lube and at Ridge Tire, under
*The same unit is installed where drivers already stop.* The two seconds come
out of the route, which drops from seven to five and is re-authored at its new
length rather than relabelled.

At twenty seconds the viewer has now seen the same unit at **three** businesses
before the first visit happens. The reading available at that point is "Uptick
installs the same thing where drivers stop, and it moves them to Joe's", not
"someone put up a QR code". **The twenty-second checkpoint is clear**, and the
screen-company risk falls with it: a unit seen once is a screen someone sold
Joe; the same unit at three trades is infrastructure somebody operates.

The route is still the weakest system at 6.5, and five seconds suits it better
than seven did.

## What the cut says to do next

1. **Establish the network before the journey, not after it.** One brief sight
   of a second placement inside the first twenty seconds would close the DIY
   reading at the point it currently opens. This is an edit change and it costs
   time that has to come from somewhere.
2. **The route is the place to take that time from.** It is seven seconds and
   scores 6.5; it is the longest weak stretch in the film, and the land either
   side of the road cannot be made to earn seven seconds without building an
   environment the film does not otherwise need.
3. The screen-company risk is reduced by the same change: a unit seen once reads
   as a screen someone sold Joe, and the same unit seen at three businesses
   reads as infrastructure somebody operates.

None of this has been applied. It is what the next revision should do, and it
should be judged on the cut again afterwards.
