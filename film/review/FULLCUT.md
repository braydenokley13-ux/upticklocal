# Full-cut review · rubric

_The final critic pass runs on the assembled film, not on shots. Sixteen categories, each scored on the stricter scale (6 prototype · 7 decent startup film · 8 professional but not special · 9 the minimum for a final film · 9.5+ signature). No category may sit below 8.5 and the overall must reach 9 before the film is called finished. An 8 means iterate._

| # | category | what a 9 means here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Story and arc | Gap → owner speaks → understands → one plan → action → two ways in → the relationship is useful → offer → pass → redeem → physical → the build → the proof → resolve, legible without narration, with nothing that has to be explained afterwards |
| 2 | The opening (Act I) | The number becomes the place with no fade, wipe or dissolve; the street is physical, morning, alive; nothing reads as a Blender test, a model viewer, a low-poly street, a previs frame or a motion study |
| 3 | Owner speaks and is understood (Acts II–III) | The sentence is the interface; meaning becomes space honestly (the flood is people, the axis is linear); no chart, no dashboard |
| 4 | One growth plan and approval (Acts IV–V) | The plan assembles from the instruments and is one statement block; approve is one tap; the plan becomes a line, the line lights only where Joe already has a way in; no network diagram |
| 5 | Two ways in (Act VI) | The text and the café screen converge on the same Offer, visibly the same object; the screen belongs inside Growth |
| 6 | The relationship is useful (Act VII) | The answer is proven to come from the approved plan by the image, not the copy; no RAG, graph or AI animation; "38 answered · 2 went to Joe" sits with provenance |
| 7 | Offer → Pass → Redeem now (Act VIII) | One transforming object — the pass carries the offer's own headline and terms; staff see it and touch nothing; **one** customer press and no interstitial; the redeemed state is unmistakable and live, and mint marks the event rather than flooding the screen |
| 8 | Digital becomes physical, the build, the proof (Acts IX–XI) | The pass goes into a pocket and through a door; the morning builds as physical life — light, people, a door — with no count over the world, no score and no particle swarm; the 21 arrives only afterwards and stands on a physical residue; "14 returned. 7 were new." is restrained; no KPI screen |
| 9 | The resolve (Act XII) | Quiet; Uptick Growth; the closing line only if earned |
| 10 | The Block | Expensive-looking: facades with depth, materials, light, contact shadows, figures with height and yaw; authored cameras with real stops; no toy read at any settle |
| 11 | Typography | One family for the film's voice (Geist), one serif for the customer's voice, mono for facts; sizes that match the ambition; nothing set small to hide |
| 12 | Colour discipline | Cream and marine as pages, ink as type; mint rationed to the events (≤ 8 moments); amber means people and nothing else |
| 13 | Motion and editing | Persist → transform between acts; one bezier, one tempo; every cut motivated by the object or the clock; no slide alternation, no cards, no arrows |
| 14 | Sound | Built into the edit: room, street, keys, ticks, the redeem note alone; the score sits under the picture; silence used as a material; nothing stock |
| 15 | Prospect-readiness | Would a serious prospective customer be shown this as a finished piece by a boutique studio, muted and with sound, on a laptop and on a wall |
| 16 | Physical / digital integration | **Non-negotiable, 9 or the film is not finished.** No Uptick surface floats in the physical world: UI lives on a phone the camera can see, on a physical panel, on signage. Screens are lit by the room, occluded by what is in front of them, blurred by the lens and motion-blurred by the shutter, because they are inside the shot. Every world crossing is authored — a rectangle that travels and lands on a real surface — never a fade. One physical residue survives the return to the page |

The critic reads the contact sheet (one frame every two seconds) and the act stills, scores each row with one sentence of evidence, names the single change that would move each sub-9 row, and gives an overall verdict from: NOT SHIPPABLE · SHIPPABLE, BUT NOT ELITE · ELITE · PROSPECT-READY.
