## Verdict

**6/10.** The premise is right and the taste is real: cream, graphite, one weight, no deck, no cards, no arrows — nothing here reads as a rejected category *except the plan*, whose five hairline row-rules and underlined "Approve" land squarely in UI-mockup chrome. "Structured understanding" half-proves: the weeks line is excellent and the 06:00–20:00 axis is genuinely linear and honest — but the film then lies on its own axis. The caption says *63 tend to answer in the morning* while the amber flood's mass sits at 16:00–19:00 (f288–312). On a product whose entire pitch is honest reading of a business, that single contradiction is disqualifying. The third failure is the payoff: the plan does not assemble from anything — between f312 and f336 the instruments vanish and a new layout cuts in, with only a stray mint stub under the "M" surviving as what looks like a render artifact. And the piece ends frozen: f360, 384 and 407 are pixel-identical, two full seconds of nothing. Meaning-becoming-space is legible for *Friday*; for *slow* it never happens, because *slow* is already parked at its final position by f144, leaving its 222 beat empty.
